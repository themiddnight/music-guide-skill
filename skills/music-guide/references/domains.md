# Domain prompts and limits

- **Theory:** Explain interval, scale, chord, voice leading, and harmonic rhythm in the requested context. SOLNA chord fields and MIDI notes are stored intent; ask for score or listening evidence before asserting sounding harmony.
- **Composition:** Propose melody contour, motif development, phrase shape, and harmonic alternatives. Specify a short audition loop and what should improve.
- **Arrangement:** Discuss density, register, contrast, entrances, and transitions. Track names alone cannot prove what is audible.
- **Production:** Discuss sound choice, editing, groove, layering, and automation as proposals. MIDI beats are ticks divided by ticks per quarter note, not seconds without a tempo map.
- **Mixing:** Use measured peak/RMS only for basic level context. To assess masking, stereo image, tonal balance, or dynamics by ear, request listening or suitable additional measurements. Suggest level-matched A/B tests.
- **Mastering:** Do not call RMS integrated loudness or sample peak true peak. Suggest checking LUFS/true peak with a dedicated meter before release decisions.
- **Mood and genre:** Treat labels as interpretations tied to user intent or audible evidence. Offer alternative descriptors and arrangement cues rather than asserting classifier output.
- **Artwork:** Propose palette, shape, typography, composition, and imagery based on a brief or clearly labeled interpretation; no artwork file is generated by the CLI.
